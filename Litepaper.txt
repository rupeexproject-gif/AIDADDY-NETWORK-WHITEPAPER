AI.DADDY NETWORK: LITEPAPER v1.0
Building the Future of AI Infrastructure
1. Introduction 
Duniya tezi se Artificial Intelligence (AI) ki taraf badh rahi hai, lekin advanced AI tools aur infrastructure aaj bhi aam entrepreneurs aur creators ki pohoch se bahar hain. AI.DADDY Network is gap ko khatam karne ke liye banaya gaya hai. Ye ek decentralized, AI-focused utility ecosystem hai, jo Base blockchain ki speed aur security ka use karke AI tools ko sabke liye accessible, scalable aur practical banata hai.
2. Our Vision
Hamara vision Web3 aur AI ki takat ko milana hai taaki ek aisa ecosystem banaya ja sake jahan businesses, developers aur creators bina kisi centralized control ke premium AI infrastructure ka fayda utha sakein.
3. Core Features (Ecosystem)
 Powered by Base Blockchain: Base network par launch hone ki wajah se AI.DADDY apne users ko lightning-fast transactions aur zero ke barabar gas fees ki guarantee deta hai.
 AI Utility Tools: Hamara platform real-world use cases par focus karta hai. Chahe wo data analysis ho, automated content creation, ya smart contract auditing—AI.DADDY ke tools sab kuch aasan banate hain.
 Decentralized Staking Dashboard: Ek secure staking mechanism jahan token holders apne tokens stake karke network ko secure kar sakte hain aur passive rewards earn kar sakte hain.
 Seamless Integration: Future mein hum decentralized exchanges (DEX) aur governance protocols ke sath direct integration plan kar rahe hain.
4. Tokenomics (Overview)
AI.DADDY sirf ek meme ya speculative token nahi hai, ye is pure ecosystem ka fuel hai.
 Utility & Access: Premium AI tools aur services ko access karne ke liye ecosystem mein AI.DADDY token ka use hoga.
 Governance: Token holders ke paas platform ke future updates aur development me voting rights honge.
 Staking Rewards: Long-term holders ko ecosystem ke revenue me se staking rewards distribute kiye jayenge.
